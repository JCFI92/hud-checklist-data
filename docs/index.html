Even Realities G2 · Even Hub app

RightSeat

Your checklist and frequencies in the glasses, so your eyes stay outside instead of down on the iPad.

What it does

  • Shows your own in-flight checklists one page at a time on the G2 display.
  • Emergency checklists open from anywhere with a long press.
  • Departure and arrival frequency cards. Type an airport ID and the FAA frequencies fill in. Data updates every 28-day cycle on its own.
  • One set of checklists per aircraft, so a DA40 and a jet don't get mixed up.
  • Tap to hide the display, tap again to bring it back where you left off.

How to start

  1. Install RightSeat from Even Hub in the Even app.
  2. Add your aircraft and type in your checklists from your POH/AFM. The app gives you blank templates, not anyone's checklist.
  3. Set your departure and arrival airports.
  4. Open RightSeat on the glasses and fly.

Controls on the glasses

SwipeNext / previous page or menu item
TapOpen a checklist · hide / show the display
Double tapBack to the menu · exit from the menu
Long pressEmergency checklists, from any screen
