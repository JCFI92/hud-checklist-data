RightSeat privacy policy

RightSeat is an Even Hub app for Even Realities G2 glasses, made by Maverick Air Group. Short version: it doesn't collect anything about you.

What stays on your phone

Your aircraft names, checklists, airports, frequencies and settings are saved only in the Even app's storage on your phone. We have no server that receives them, and we can't see them.

Network permission

RightSeat uses the network permission for one thing: downloading a public file of FAA airport frequencies from jcfi92.github.io when your phone is online. The request contains nothing about you, your aircraft or your checklists. The data comes from the FAA's public 28-day NASR release. GitHub, which hosts the file, may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) under GitHub's privacy statement.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.
  • No microphone, camera, location or photo access.
  • Nothing is sold or shared.

Deleting your data

Uninstall RightSeat from the Even app, or delete your aircraft inside the app. That removes everything RightSeat saved.

Terms

RightSeat is a training and convenience aid, provided as is, with no warranty. It does not replace the aircraft POH/AFM, approved checklists or official frequency sources. The pilot in command is responsible for verifying all information and for the use of any portable electronic device in the aircraft.
